# SUPPLEMENTARY PROMPT: ENVIRONMENT & JUMPSCARE ENGINE DIRECTIVES

## 1. ENVIRONMENTAL SPECIFICATIONS: PHOTOREALISTIC ABANDONED HOUSE
* **Visual Engine Target:** Photorealistic rendering (Unreal Engine 5 Nanite/Lumen standard).
* **Asset Quality:** High-density 3D scans (Megascans), physically-based rendering (PBR) materials with multi-layered wear.

### Architectural & Texture Details:
* **The Structure:** A Victorian-style residential house rotting from the inside. Peeling wallpaper revealing damp, mold-infested drywall.
* **Lighting:** Volumetric dust motes cutting through broken window shutters. Ray-traced, ultra-sharp dynamic shadows cast by the player's flashlight.
* **Surfaces:** Highly detailed puddle reflections on decaying hardwood floors. Layered grime, water stains dripping down walls, and cracked porcelain surfaces in the bathroom.

---

## 2. MULTI-AGENT ARCHITECTURE DIRECTIVE: THE JUMPSCARE SQUAD
Assign a dedicated sub-series of AI agents exclusively to design, time, and execute jumpscares.

### Agent 01: Visual Shock Designer
* **Task:** Create horrific, high-contrast entity models.
* **Execution:** Jumpscare entities must feature uncanny, distorted human anatomy, unhinged jaws, and rapid, twitching animations (60 FPS to 0 FPS glitch framing).

### Agent 02: Audio Impact Engineer
* **Task:** Handle the auditory shock value.
* **Execution:** Maximize dynamic range. Jumpscares must be preceded by absolute silence (0 dB), instantly shattered by a localized, clipping audio blast (binaural screech, breaking bones, or metallic tearing).

### Agent 03: Telemetry & Timing Orchestrator
* **Task:** Detect the exact micro-second to launch the attack.
* **Execution:** Monitor player camera angle (FOV) and movement speed. Trigger the jumpscare *exactly* when the player is hyper-focused on an interaction or turning a sharp corner to guarantee maximum vulnerability.

---

## 3. MANDATORY CORE ASSIGNMENT
> **CRITICAL INSTRUCTION:** Combine the photorealistic environment with the jumpscare engine. The horror relies entirely on the contrast between the grounded, ultra-realistic setting and the sudden, violent disruption of reality caused by the audio-visual shocks. Do not compromise on graphic fidelity.
